Pricing

Pricing that reads like a catalog.

Every application carries its price on the shelf, pass-through costs appear at their real rates, and a quote is a configuration you can read line by line.

Get a configured quote See the catalog

How a Freehold bill is structured.

A configured deployment prices as a bill of materials you can actually read:

Configured deployment · bill of materialsevery line visible
LineWhat it coversHow it recurs
Base deployment The standard app library (chat, retrieval, coding assistant), the governance layer, and managed operations under the SLA. Subscription, with a per-seat governance license.
Add-on applications Applications selected from the catalog beyond the standard library, each priced individually on the shelf. Per-app subscription. Add or drop as needs change.
Onboarding A bounded, fixed-scope engagement: identity federation, corpus ingestion, posture setup, hardware install where applicable. One-time, at a fixed price agreed up front.
Pass-throughs Hardware on your bill of materials at vendor pricing; the NVIDIA AI Enterprise entitlement per GPU at its published rate; third-party model usage under your own keys at provider rates. As incurred, itemized at their real rates.

The fee is the same in every posture. Governance, catalog, and operations are what we charge for, whether your models run on your hardware, in your VPC, or behind governed APIs. Our income stays flat across all three, which frees the posture advice to follow your numbers.

What moves the price.

Seats

Who's governed

The governance license follows people, because that's what the layer does: identity, access, budgets, and audit per member.

Apps

What's on your shelf

More applications from the catalog, higher subscription. Each one priced individually, visible before you add it.

Isolation posture

How strict you need to be

Stricter isolation postures cost more: they're harder to operate and carry fewer conveniences. A full air gap is the top of that ladder, and the ladder is priced honestly.

Commitments

Commitments you can hold us to.

  • The deployed product is whole. Everything your employees open is complete, and new capability appears on the catalog shelf, in the open.
  • Model usage under your keys stays between you and your providers. Our fee holds flat however your prompt volume grows.
  • Onboarding has a fixed scope and a fixed price. Genuinely custom work gets named as such and priced as the deliberate exception it is.
  • Your bill of materials shows real rates: hardware at vendor pricing, entitlements at published list.
