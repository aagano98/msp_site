Pricing

Priced like a catalog. Because it is one.

In a market where every alternative prices by sales call, our commitment is structural transparency: every application carries its price on the shelf, pass-through costs appear as what they are, and nothing in the deployed product is locked, teased, or interrupted.

How a Freehold bill is structured.

A configured deployment prices as a bill of materials you can actually read:

LineWhat it coversHow it recurs
Base deployment The standard app library (chat, retrieval, coding assistant), the governance layer, and managed operations under the SLA. Subscription, with a per-seat governance license.
Add-on applications Applications selected from the catalog beyond the standard library, each priced individually on the shelf. Per-app subscription. Add or drop as needs change.
Onboarding A bounded, fixed-scope engagement: identity federation, corpus ingestion, posture setup, hardware install where applicable. One-time, fixed price. Not an open-ended services meter.
Pass-throughs Hardware on your bill of materials at vendor pricing; the NVIDIA AI Enterprise entitlement per GPU at its published rate; third-party model usage under your own keys at provider rates. As incurred, visible as what they are. We don't wrap margin in fog.

The fee is the same in every posture. Governance, catalog, and operations are what we charge for, whether your models run on your hardware, in your VPC, or behind governed APIs. We don't skim tokens and we don't earn extra when you buy hardware, which is why our posture advice can follow your numbers instead of our incentives.

What moves the price.

Seats

Who's governed

The governance license follows people, because that's what the layer does: identity, access, budgets, and audit per member.

Apps

What's on your shelf

More applications from the catalog, higher subscription. Each one priced individually, visible before you add it.

Isolation posture

How strict you need to be

Stricter isolation postures cost more: they're harder to operate and carry fewer conveniences. A full air gap is the top of that ladder, and the ladder is priced honestly.

What you won't find

Things we've structurally declined to do.

  • No locked features in the deployed product. What's deployed is whole. Upsell happens on the catalog shelf, in the open, not inside your employees' workflow.
  • No per-token margin. Model usage under your keys is between you and your providers. Our fee doesn't grow when your prompts do.
  • No open-ended services meter. Onboarding is fixed-scope by design. If a request is genuinely custom, we'll say so and price it as the deliberate exception it is.
  • No pricing-by-opacity. Hardware and entitlements appear on your bill of materials at their real rates.
