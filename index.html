free·hold/ˈfriː.hoʊld/ · noun · property law An estate held outright and indefinitely, free of any landlord. The opposite of a leasehold, in which the occupant pays rent on the owner's terms — and the asset reverts to the owner when the lease ends.

AI infrastructure has the same two tenures.

Most companies hold their AI as a leasehold and don't call it that. Freehold exists because ownership is now practical for companies that will never hire a platform team.

Sovereignty as an architectural property.

Most vendors promise your data is safe by contract. We built a system that can't reach it in the first place — and prove it with injected faults, not assurances.

Configuration is the only control path

Every deployment converges to a versioned, declarative config held in your perimeter — validated, signed, and reconciled by a proven GitOps engine. Drift is detected and reverted. The audit trail is the change history itself.

Structural data-plane separation

We operate the control plane and are architecturally unable to read the data plane. Telemetry carries control-plane fields only — a constraint that contractually binds our subcontractors too. "We run the plumbing, we never see your data" is a verifiable system property.

An egress ladder, not a blunt air gap

Isolation posture is configurable: full air gap, governed egress — user-invoked web search, docs lookup, whitelisted APIs, all logged — or fully connected. Destination control with an audit trail beats a checkbox.

Fault-proven enforcement

Every governance control — access, quota, egress, resilience — is validated by an injected-fault test suite and shown to fail visibly and recover cleanly. Enforcement is demonstrated, not asserted, and re-proven on every substrate change.

Built on the certified stack. We add the layer it's missing.

Model serving and scheduling run on NVIDIA AI Enterprise behind a clean interface — defect liability stays with the vendor, certification ports across hardware lines, and our engineering concentrates on the governance and experience layer the raw stack doesn't provide.
