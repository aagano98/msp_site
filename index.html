Every company's AI stack is three layers. You shouldn't have to build any of them.

The models doing the work, the rules about who can use which model and how, and the applications employees actually open. Enterprises with a platform team weld those layers into something that fits them. Everyone else buys frontier seats one login at a time and lives with the terms attached. Freehold is the third option: all three layers, pre-assembled, delivered as a configured deployment.

One product. Three places models can run.

The platform, your console, gateway, applications, and member memory, always runs under your control. The models are the layer that moves: each workload runs wherever its economics and constraints point, and can be moved later without a re-integration project.

On-premises

Your hardware, your building

Certified enterprise AI systems from Dell, HPE, or Lenovo, on your balance sheet, installed and operated under our SLA. The strongest sovereignty and the best economics at high sustained volume.

strongest sovereignty
Your cloud

A VPC in your name

GPU capacity rented in your own cloud tenancy. No capex, data stays in your tenancy. Don't have a cloud footprint? We stand one up in your name as part of onboarding.

control without a data center
Governed API

Frontier & open models, your keys

Third-party providers reached through your own governed gateway, under your own keys, with per-person budgets and a content-free audit. Start today, no procurement cycle.

fastest start

Mixing postures is the point. One group's chat can run on a local model while another group's runs against a frontier provider under your key with an allowance, falling back to the local model when it's spent. Moving a workload between postures changes where its model runs and nothing else.

Sovereignty as an architectural property.

Not a contract clause: a system property. Your data staying inside your perimeter is the design constraint the whole product is built to hold, and where data does cross a boundary, the crossing is explicit, permitted, and labelled.

One control path

Config in, everything else out

Every change is versioned, signed configuration, reconciled by a proven GitOps engine. Drift is detected and reverted. The audit trail is the change history.

Structural separation

We never see your data

We operate the control plane and are architecturally unable to read the data plane. Prompts, documents, and weights are never visible to us, and that constraint contractually binds our subcontractors too.

Fault-proven

Tested by breaking it

Every enforcement control is validated by injected-fault tests: shown to fail visibly and recover cleanly before we claim it. The audit trail is content-free by construction.

How buying works.

A configured deployment, not a platform project. It arrives working, and the operational machinery stays on our side so you never have to learn it.

Configure

Select applications from the catalog, models from the curated menu, and the posture mix that fits your constraints. Every app carries its price on the shelf. A deployment is a selection, not a specification document.

Bounded onboarding

A fixed-scope engagement: identity federation with your IdP, corpus ingestion for retrieval, hardware install where your posture includes it. No open-ended integration project, no platform team required on your side.

Operated, under one SLA

We run the platform and gate every update; hardware vendors deliver field service as our subcontractors, never as a second vendor you manage. Your recourse is always us. The catalog keeps growing while you run.
